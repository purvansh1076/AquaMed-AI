## 4. Human-in-the-Loop Governance Policy

The project documentation explicitly affirms that AquaMedi AI is an **intelligent decision-support system**:
- Water allocation schedules generated by the LP allocator or multi-agent mediation engine require formal approval by a designated **Irrigation Officer** before canal gate operations.
- The software does not connect directly to physical PLC/SCADA canal actuators.
- Dispute mediation outputs provide structured compromise options for Water User Associations (WUAs) rather than legally binding autonomous decrees.
